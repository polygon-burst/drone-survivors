DEMO STAGE

  The setting is a random planet in a random solar system, where 
drones from autonomous mining facilities of the companies deepSPACE 
and ShengLong (originating from the defunct planet Terra) are 
battling each other.

  Your goal as a Vogon Administrator Drone is to collect so called 
'artifacts' left behind by an ancient civilization, and eliminate 
any problems that may arise along the way.
